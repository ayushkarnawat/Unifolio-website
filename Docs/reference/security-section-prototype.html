Security section redesign

Built from pages 9 and 10 of the Unifolio website redesign PDF. The scroll-driven security section becomes one static section. The vault drawing from the PDF is cut apart and animated like the hero and product illustrations, the headline keeps the current "money" banknote animation, the four principles get small icons taken from your pencil sketches (moving), and the hero's "Locked at rest / in transit / to you" block moves here, centred. Nothing on the website has been changed yet.

How it works

A single static security section. It replaces the current scroll-driven one entirely (the pinned vault, the scroll states, the eyes and password tricks, and the separate mobile dock).

Headline
No small label and no line under it. "We take your data as seriously as you take your money." Same wording as now. The word "money" keeps the current animation: the letters fold into a banknote, two more bills fan out behind it, they hold, fold back, and the word returns. It replays every few seconds while the section is on screen.
Vault
Vertically centred next to the text. The drawing from the PDF, cut into pieces: the vault door, the wheel (it turns slowly back and forth, around a still logo), the six asset cards (they float and tilt), green streaks that flow from the door to each card, small pings on the dots, and a soft glow that pulses behind the logo. The handwritten "Your data stays protected. Always." stays as drawn.
Four principles
Read only, never save passwords, don’t sell your data, stored in India, in a two by two grid. Each principle has a small icon cut from the four sketches you supplied (the eye, the padlock, the no-sign, the cloud-and-tick shield), so they carry the same pencil style as the vault, at the same small size as the first version. They move: the eye blinks and glances, the padlock rattles, the no-sign pulses with a ring, the shield (from the servers sketch, with the cloud and tick) rises and glows green: the eye looks around and blinks, the lock opens and shuts, the document gets struck through, the pin bobs over a ping.
Trust block
The hero’s block, copied here as it is and centred, floating on the page with no card behind it: a small picture, a heading and one line, cycling through Locked at rest, Locked in transit, Locked to you. It stays in the hero too for now (you will replace it there later).
Tablet
My proposal, not in the PDF. The headline and line on top, then the vault, then the four principles, then the trust block.
Phone
Headline, vault, then one centred block that slides on its own every 2.5 seconds (or swipe it, no dots): the four principles, then Locked at rest, in transit and to you. The four principles are no longer a separate list.

Where the PDF disagrees with itself (what I used)

  1. PrinciplesThe design page (9) shows eight items (Encrypted in transit, Restricted access, Secure statement handling, You’re in control, No selling your data, Transparent permissions, Account deletion) plus a “Read our privacy policy” card. The copy page (10) has only four: Read only, never save passwords, don’t sell your data, stored in India. I used the copy page’s four, in the design’s two-column style.
  2. Section 3The design shows a “Built with trusted partners” strip; the copy page has the three “Locked” lines. As you asked, I used the hero’s security block instead, with the hero’s own wording (the DPDP sentence now stays on one line).
  3. WordingCopy page: “Locked at rest: AES-256 encryption protects your data.” and “Locked to you: You approve access. You can revoke it anytime. Built for India’s DPDP Act.” The hero block says something shorter. I kept the hero wording because you said to bring it as it is.
  4. TypoThe copy page says “RBIs data localisation”. I wrote “RBI’s”.
  5. NavigationThe mockup nav shows text links (Product, Security, FAQs). I have not touched navigation.
