About section redesign

Built from pages 11 and 12 of the Unifolio website redesign PDF. The scroll-driven About section (the envelope and the editorial morph) becomes one static section: a headline, one line of copy, a new animated illustration, and a closing statement. The illustration is new, from the sketch you supplied: four family members, each looking at a part of the family's finances, all connected to one shared dashboard. Nothing on the website has been changed yet.

How it works

One static section with no scroll animation. It replaces the current About section entirely (the pinned envelope that unfolds into the editorial text as you scroll).

Headline
“Your family’s wealth is connected. Your understanding should be, too.” Two lines, the second in green.
Copy
The line “Unifolio brings your family’s finance together, helping you see the bigger picture.” sits under the headline on the left, as in the earlier version. Both sentences try to stay on one line: the text shrinks to fit, down to a minimum size, and only wraps if it would otherwise get too small. On desktop the left column is narrow, so that sentence usually wraps to two lines at a readable size.
Illustration
Your sketch, animated. Four family members around one shared dashboard: one checks investments on a phone, one looks at bank accounts on a laptop, one looks at the family home, one ticks off financial goals. Dashed green lines carry glowing dots from each person to the middle, and each time one arrives the middle gives a soft ring. The dashboard breathes, a green highlight circles its donut, a small ping pulses on each person's screen, and everyone floats very gently. It is cut from your drawing, the same way as the other illustrations.
Closing statement
“Better clarity. Smarter decisions.” with “Understand what you own, what’s working, and what comes next.” centred below, joined by a dotted line, with the second sentence also on one line and a green underline that draws in.
Tablet and phone
On tablet the headline is sized so each of its two lines, including “Your understanding should be, too.”, stays on one line. Everything stacks and centres: headline, the line, illustration, closing statement. The sentences stay on one line while they can be at least 14px (11px for the closing one), otherwise they wrap.
