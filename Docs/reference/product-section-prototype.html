How it works

A new, lighter Product section. It replaces the current one entirely (the "Don't just see your wealth. Understand it." headline, the five hover-and-flip bento cards and the mobile card carousel).

Header
The headline from the copy page with "understanding it." in green. No small label and no line under it.
Illustration
The PDF's artwork: six asset cards (Mutual Funds, Stocks, Bank Accounts on the left; Real Estate, PMS / AIFs, Gold on the right) feeding a laptop that shows the dashboard. In motion: the cards float and tilt a little, the arrows have bright dots travelling into the laptop, the screen glows softly as they arrive, a green highlight circles the donut, a dot rides the chart line, and the three lines above the laptop pulse.
Cards
Three cards: 01 Track, 02 Analyse, 03 Plan, each with its own animated illustration (the stack of statements, the rising bars, the hill with the flag) that drifts slowly. Hovering a card lifts it slightly.
Tablet
Same design as the PDF: illustration on top, then the three cards side by side, just scaled to fit.
Phone
My proposal, not in the PDF. The illustration scales down to fit, then the three cards sit in a swipeable carousel with arrows and pills (the next card peeks in, and it advances by itself).

Where the PDF disagrees with itself (what I used)

  1. HeadlineThe mockup says "Your family's wealth, understood." but the copy page says "From seeing your wealth to understanding it." I used the copy page. The small label and the line under the headline are removed, as you asked.
  2. Card labelsMockup: AGGREGATE / ANALYSE / PLAN. Copy page: TRACK / ANALYSE / PLAN. I used the copy page.
  3. Card 1Mockup title and text are the same as the copy page, except the mockup's body says "mutual funds, stocks, bank accounts, real estate and more" and the copy page says "mutual funds, stocks, real estate, gold and more". I used the copy page.
  4. Card 2Mockup: "Know what's really working." Understand returns, overlap, fees and risk without digging through spreadsheets. Copy page: "Understand what's really happening." Go beyond portfolio value… I used the copy page.
  5. Card 3Same title. The mockup body is a slightly shorter version of the copy page's. I used the copy page.
  6. IllustrationThe artwork shows Bank Accounts and PMS / AIFs tiles, and the copy lists gold but not bank accounts. The PDF says illustrations are indicative, so I kept the artwork exactly as drawn.
  7. NavigationThe mockup's top bar shows text links (Product, Security, FAQs) and a "Join the waitlist" button, while the hero mockup (page 3) keeps the current icon bar. I have not touched navigation in this section.
  8. Page 8"Product Section 2" (real app screenshots of unique features and analytics) is marked "to be implemented in the future", so it is not in this artifact.

Questions before I build this

  1. Headline. Is the headline "From seeing your wealth to understanding it." (copy page) right? The small label and the line under it are already removed.
  2. Card labels. TRACK (copy page) or AGGREGATE (mockup) on the first card?
  3. Card 2 title. "Understand what's really happening." (copy page) or "Know what's really working." (mockup)?
  4. What goes away. The old section's five cards (Skip the dashboards. Just ask., See everything, Understand what you own, Know your risk, Plan ahead), their hover and flip effects and the scroll links that point at them would be removed. Is that right?
  5. Tablet and phone. Tablet keeps the PDF design (three cards in a row, scaled down). Phone gets the carousel. Does that work for you?
  6. Illustration on phones. The artwork is wide, so on a phone its labels are small. I can keep it as is, or rearrange the cards into a tighter group around the laptop. Which?
What I would check first

Watch the illustration on desktop for a few seconds (the arrows, the screen glow and the donut highlight), then look at the phone carousel. Once you are happy I will write the plan and the Anti Gravity prompt for this batch, the same way as for the hero and the waitlist.
